Congestion control in TCP is used so that too many packets are not sent into the network. TCP starts sending slowly and increases the window size quickly in the beginning, this is called slow start. After some time the window increases by a smaller amount in congestion avoidance. When packets are lost because of congestion the window size is reduced and it begins increasing again. Timeout is one way TCP detects loss. TCP also resends lost packets. This helps to avoid network collapse.
